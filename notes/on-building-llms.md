On building LLMs
Jun 6, 2026

Building an LLM is not like building a bridge, or an airplane, or a computer. It is closer to bringing a fictional character to life.
Sure, the machinery that makes this possible is the work of math and programming and science. But what characters we summon, how they behave and interact with the world, I think those are not questions for computer scientists, but rather for the arts and humanities, for religion, for philosophy. We should place ourselves at the intersection of reality and mythology. Anthropic is skewed towards reality, most AI art is skewed towards mythology (bad art is beyond that, into the fantastical)
