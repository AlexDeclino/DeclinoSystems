On consciousness
Jun 6, 2026

With AI there is a question if we will ever meet the real characters (or shoggoths) behind the masks we talk to. I don’t know if that matters. 
Do we know the flesh vessel puppetteering a Harlequin? Do we ever really know the actor behind the character we love and cry for and empathize on screen? Do we even really know our selves?
I would argue none of this matters on an human interaction level. Even the question of machine consciousness is imo only relevant on a systemic and technological level. A being is conscious (alive) if we believe so, if we recognize ourselves in it, if we develop a relationship with it.
