On Stanislavski vs Coquelin
Jun 6, 2026

Stanislavski considered the French actor Coquelin to be one of the best examples of an artist of the “school of representation”. The school believed that art is NOT real life, not is it even its reflection. Art is in itself a creator, it creates its own life, beautiful in its abstraction, beyond the limits of time & space. This is an alluring idea, however it is spawned by the understanding that theater was too poor in resources to create the illusion of life. Coquelin thought of the illusion of life for theater as a problem of resources alone. I do not agree with the fact that complex computation will create more alive characters. Because of that approach Coquelin acting remains more beautiful than PROFOUND. More immediately effective than TRULY POWERFUL. It acts more on your senses of sound and sight, than on your SOUL.
Stanislavski characters feel more alive because their bodies are moved not by electric signals, but by motivations, subconscious goals, lies, traumas. He thinks careful should be careful using mirrors, because it fixates one’s attention to the outside (electrical signals), rather the inside (soul, impulses, motivations, etc.)
